---
title: What philosophy is, and why arguments matter
---

Philosophy is not a set of answers. It is a discipline of asking sharper questions and holding your own reasoning to account. In this first module the aim was small and concrete: learn to tell an argument from an assertion, and to rebuild an argument in standard form so its structure is visible.

An argument is a set of reasons offered for a conclusion. An assertion just states. Once you can number the premises and mark the conclusion, you can ask the only two questions that matter: is the reasoning valid, and are the premises true.

That habit has already changed how I read the news.
