---
title: 'Hot vs cold compost: a first controlled experiment'
---

## Introduction
Does an actively managed compost pile reach a higher temperature than an unmanaged control?

## Method
Two equal-volume piles were built on the same day at Voi. The hot pile was balanced for carbon and nitrogen, turned, and kept moist. The cold pile was left unmanaged. Temperature was recorded daily at a fixed depth.

## Results
The hot pile peaked near 66 degrees on day 5 before cooling, while the control held around 24 degrees throughout. See the chart in the Field room.

## Discussion
The result matches the microbial account of hot composting. The main limitation was a single replicate per treatment.
